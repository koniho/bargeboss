Built-in image generation; 4×2 candidate atlas. References: industrial-isometric-concept.png (style), factory-pump-house-v2.png (accepted material language), ground-industrial-v1.png (ground perspective).

Create one coherent production-candidate 4-column by 2-row sprite atlas of EIGHT parkland elements for Barge Boss's Montrose Harbor. Transparent background, every sprite separate with generous empty alpha gutters, no titles or labels or borders. Reference1 industrial-isometric-concept is STYLE / MATERIAL / LIGHTING reference, NOT a background to reproduce. Reference2 accepted factory-pump-house-v2 is exact chunky prerendered cartoon material language. Reference3 accepted ground-industrial-v1 supplies footprint perspective and understated ground texture. Camera fixed ORTHOGRAPHIC 35.264 degrees above ground, isometric axes +/-30deg, vertical trunks vertical. Smooth prerendered 3D-looking painted sprites, NOT pixel art, not photographic foliage. Cool muted green foliage, subdued gray limestone, small warm upper-left highlights, cool right-side shading. Keep daylight albedo brightness comparable to reference2, scene darkness is applied later in game. Avoid yellow/neon foliage.
Layout slots left-to-right, top-to-bottom: 0 a perfectly flat lawn diamond with broad subtle blue-green tonal variations, very smooth sparse texture; 1 another perfectly flat same-size lawn diamond with restrained moss-green patches, no grass tufts or flowers or bald brown spots; 2 grouped three mature broadleaf park trees of DIFFERENT heights, broad sculpted rounded canopy masses with only minimal large leaf clusters, brown trunks and no base/plinth; 3 grouped two shorter broadleaf trees plus one compact shrub, same style no base/plinth; 4 low limestone shoreline cluster of 5 large angular boulders, cool slate-gray sides and muted warm gray tops; 5 long low limestone riprap cluster roughly 2:1 elongated diagonally up-right with 7 chunky rocks; 6 a curved low limestone corner cluster oriented around a harbor entrance, no soil or water; 7 long low limestone riprap cluster elongated diagonally down-right with 7 chunky rocks. Both long strips are separately authored orientations from the same fixed camera, NOT rotated pictures. Rocks should read as heavy irregular limestone blocks, broad facets and little cracking, not pebble noise.
Each sprite centered within its own equal square atlas cell with at least 30px invisible margin. Lawn diamond is width 80% of cell, height 46.2% of cell, center (.5,.6), vertices about (.1,.6),(.5,.369),(.9,.6),(.5,.831). Lawns are zero-thickness ground surfaces, no vertical edge faces, no cast shadow, no soil blocks. All trees fit within their cells, have visible trunk roots, ground plane pivot (.5,.85). Rocks low profile, ground footprint center (.5,.70). Use opaque material interiors and clean antialiased alpha silhouettes. NO cast shadows outside objects, NO ground patches beneath trees or rocks, NO baked water, foam, wakes, smoke, glowing lamps, environment or text. This is a composable small-town lakefront park extension of the industrial game's art, coherent with the concept but simpler than its busy factories.
