Smooth high-resolution prerendered isometric game sprite, fixed orthographic camera 35.264 degrees above ground and square base edges projected at 30 degrees to horizontal. Chunky readable proportions, restrained rusty detail, warm upper-left key. One complete isolated object, centered, transparent alpha background, generous 10 percent clear margin on ALL sides. No ground plane outside the plinth, no water, waves, glow halo, smoke, people, text or detached cast shadow. Neutral unlit glass for independently animated lighting. Keep tower vertical and foundation square.
