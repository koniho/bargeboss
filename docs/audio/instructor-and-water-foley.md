# Instructor contact and water Foley

Original synthesized audio generated by `tools/generate-water-audio.py`, without
recorded samples or external dependencies. Inputs: a short muffled tug/fender
thud, a small splash and a larger splash. All files are mono, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM.

- `tug-fender-thud-v1.wav`: 0.34 s; damped low hull modes and filtered rubber-like
  compression. No metallic rattle. Peak 0.55.
- `water-splash-small-v1.wav`: 0.63 s; low water body, broken sheet, wash and
  scattered decaying bubble/droplet modes. Peak 0.34.
- `water-splash-large-v1.wav`: 0.92 s; broader water body and more returning drops.
  Peak 0.49.

Fixed random seeds provide reproducible source assets. DC removal, edge fades
and peak headroom prevent digital clipping and hard sample boundaries. These are
synthesized approximations, not field recordings; final perceived realism and
phone speaker balance still require listening review.

Shared Java selects the fender thud for tug-to-tug contact at every audible
severity. Collision spray emits one splash event when a packet first returns to
the water; small/large selection uses its existing impact power. Existing bounded
contact pools, fixed-step cooldowns and radio priority prevent chatter. Native
hosts decode the same shared cue manifest; iOS now uses its count instead of a
hard-coded five-entry array. No phone installation was performed in this round.
