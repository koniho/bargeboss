# Montrose beach and beacon extension

The Montrose stage now continues north past the sanctuary to the crescent beach and hooked lake breakwater. The original marina, dock rows, rescue locations and inlet remain in their existing coordinates. The larger play bounds also drive the north-up overview; open water provides a continuous route around the peninsula and beacon into the beach bay.

Reference was inspected in Google Maps through native Chrome automation on 2026-11-20: satellite view of the beach/harbor and the place-photo covers for [Montrose Harbor Beacon](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Montrose+Harbor+Beacon/@41.9686626,-87.6310371,17z) and [The Dock at Montrose Beach](https://www.google.com/maps/place/The+Dock+at+Montrose+Beach/@41.964857,-87.63615,17z). The user-supplied satellite reference guided the overall extent. Observed features include the sandy crescent, grass/dune patches, long concrete breakwater with a hooked head and red lattice beacon, rear beach path, low flat-roof pavilion, detached restroom and small kayak concession. Buildings are representative geometry, not surveyed replicas. No Google image pixels are shipped as game textures.

The northern shoreline is appended to the single `MontroseHarbor` coast. Collision, water triangles, solid-shore cutouts and chart boundaries derive from it. Sand, dune marks and paths are baked into existing bounded ground tiles. Three low shared rigid models and the existing red lattice beacon art enter the shared landmark depth merge. The hoist and geographic landmarks now compare their actual depths before drawing. Terrain uses distinct cache keys per distance tier; no full-stage raster is created. Half, quarter and eighth-size local layers share original geometry while reducing distant terrain raster requests to 4.4–8.6 MB in the reviewed wide views. Close views retain the original density.

North-lake swell is enabled independently of the marina exposure field. The beach bay gets reduced swell and the lake side of the beacon gets full exposure. Harbor water stays calm. This is an authored exposure field, not a bathymetric or wind-refraction simulation.

`MontroseBeachTest` checks the navigable route, solid beach/buildings/breakwater, overview direction, shore-mask coverage, triangulated area and wave exposure. `MontroseBeachReview` exports actual shared-renderer overview, beach, beacon, pavilion and retained-inlet views. `ShoreOcclusionPixelTest montrose` probes all 32 hull headings on four new edges. Developer scenes `montrose-beach` and `montrose-beacon` support native review; `montrose-marina` retains the old overview camera anchor for comparable performance captures.
