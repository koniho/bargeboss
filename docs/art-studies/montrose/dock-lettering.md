# Montrose dock letters

The user-supplied [dock map](dock-lettering-reference.png) is authoritative.

- A–I follow the inner peninsula bank from its first long dock toward the east and north.
- J–O continue along the north bank from east to west.
- Star docks are P (upper left), R (upper right), S (lower left), T (lower middle), and U (lower right).
- Q is deliberately absent from the reference. Service and harbor-mouth piers retain gates without letter plaques.

`MontroseShoreArt` maps these names to the existing shared dock/access geometry. Letter geometry comes from the shared `Lettering` alphabet and is projected onto an upright sign mounted above each gate on a short steel post. The signs use the fixed world diagonal facing the shared isometric camera, so gates viewed edge-on still have readable letters.

The five star docks are isolated eight-arm floats, with no land spines, ramps or gates. Their P/R/S/T/U letters sit on small posts at the central hubs, rendered with the hub pedestals at vessel depth. Only the 15 main docks and four service/inlet access points have shore gates (19 total). The connected shore promenade serves those shore-connected structures.
