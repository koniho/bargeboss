# Montrose parkland v2 candidate

Eight candidates use restrained cool lawn, grouped trees with varied heights, and chunky gray limestone banks. These are a coherent park extension of the industrial concept, not a replacement for the harbor geometry. Port-side returning entrance rocks should use frames 4/5/6/7 as appropriate; frame 7 is separately authored for the opposite diagonal, not a rotated isometric bitmap.

Built-in imagegen used the industrial concept, accepted pump house and ground tile as references. The exact prompt and alpha correction are in [montrose-park-v2.md](../../../art-prompts/montrose-park-v2.md). The opaque first result is retained for provenance; **use `parkland-atlas-v2-alpha.png`**, 1774×887, 4×2. Every rounded cell has alpha>=128 bounds inside its gutters, nearest gap22px. Pixels: alpha0=1,197,478, alpha1..254=374,521, alpha255=1,539. Material interiors retain the generated near-opaque alpha; no threshold/paintout was applied to source art. Composites show no colored halos despite misleading transparent-image previews.

`metadata.json` contains frame order, source rules, lawn vertices, estimated root/rock pivots, and explicit empty lighting/smoke/steam inventories. No fixtures are visible. Lawn pivots and vertices were pixel measured; tree/rock footprint centers are estimates because their real model geometry is unavailable. Lawn axes measure approximately29.4° rather than requested30°: close, not mathematically exact.

`parkland-composites-v2.png` compares source and existing scenery RGB grade(.48,.54,.63) on water, light and dark backgrounds. Existing grading is appropriate for first integration: dark cool greens and slate rocks remain distinct, and the lawn no longer has bright yellow speckles. Avoid additionally baking darkness into the source. Leaves still have small lobes; they read as larger canopy masses at gameplay size, but final actual-render review should decide whether further simplification is needed.

`parkland-lawn-joins-v2.png` uses unchanged source pixels in a3×3 arrangement. Generated lawn is much quieter than v1 but is **not guaranteed seamless**: approximately1px diagonal gaps and broad patch contrast remain in direct adjacency. Match measured diamond vertices, use existing solid land underfill at tiny alpha seams, and favor mostly frame0 with occasional frame1 rather than strict checkerboarding. Do not enlarge water boundaries or use sprite alpha as collisions to hide gaps. Low-rock joins are irregular overlap clusters, not precisely endpoint-matched structural modules. Keep overlaps shallow and use the corner cluster at turns.

Independent contact/cast masks are in `parkland-shadow-v2.png`, generated by `ReviewPark.java` using the accepted alpha-derived shadow pipeline:128px cells,96px source,16px padding, alpha64 cutoff, sigma1.0/3.2. Lawn slots are intentionally empty. Every mask cell edge is alpha0. This is a silhouette projection, not a recovered3-D model. Root owns runtime registration, receiving-plane clipping and native affine review; candidates alone do not establish those checks.

java -Djava.awt.headless=true -cp build/park-review ReviewPark

No gameplay, atlas registration, collision geometry or production assets were changed by this subtask.
